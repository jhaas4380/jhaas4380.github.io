JARVIS

A private, personal assistant app.

JARVIS is a personal assistant that runs on its owner's own Windows PC. It is used by one person only and is not offered to the public.

With its owner's permission, JARVIS reads their Google Calendar to answer questions such as "what's on tomorrow?" and to spot double bookings. It can prepare new events or changes, but nothing is written to the calendar until the owner approves that exact change.

How JARVIS handles Google user data is described in the privacy policy.

JARVIS is a personal project and is not affiliated with Google or Anthropic.
